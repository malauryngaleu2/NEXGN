The problem

The resume is a 30-year-old tool still gatekeeping a world it was never built for.

Today's candidates are articulate, creative, and driven — but they're filtered out before a human ever sees them. A one-page document can't capture how someone communicates, leads a room, or handles pressure. And recruiters, buried in hundreds of identical applications, don't have the time or signal to find who's actually right. The system is broken on both sides.

✕
Filtered by format
ATS systems reject qualified people before a recruiter ever reads their name.
◎
Personality is invisible
A resume tells you what someone did. It tells you nothing about who they are.
◷
Hiring takes too long
Average time-to-hire is 6–8 weeks. Most candidates are gone by week three.
↓
Bad hires are expensive
Culture-fit failures cost 1.5–2x annual salary. You can't measure fit from a PDF.

What is NEXGN

A recruiting platform built around one idea: let people speak for themselves.

NEXGN lets candidates record a short video introduction — no scripts, no studio, no stress. Recruiters scroll through real people in real time and connect directly with who stands out. Think of it as the first recruiting platform designed for how people actually communicate: fast, visual, and human. No applications. No cover letters. No black holes.

▶
60-second videos

⊞
